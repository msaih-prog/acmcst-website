About the Conference

Welcome to ACMCST 2027

The 2nd International Conference on Advanced Communications with Modern Circuits and Systems Technologies

The ACMCST 2027 is an international event organized by the MISET Research Laboratory at Sultan Moulay Slimane University, Béni Mellal, Morocco.

This conference brings together renowned researchers, experts, and professionals in the fields of advanced communication technologies, modern circuits, and intelligent systems. Its primary objective is to explore the latest advances in electronic engineering, telecommunications, advanced technologies, and smart systems.

ACMCST 2027 provides an interactive platform for participants to share ideas, present research findings, and exchange experiences. The conference welcomes researchers, academics, engineers, industry professionals, and students from around the world.

With a strong emphasis on networking, collaboration, and knowledge exchange, ACMCST 2027 represents an excellent opportunity for participants to stay informed about recent developments and emerging trends in communication technologies, electronic systems, and multidisciplinary engineering fields.
