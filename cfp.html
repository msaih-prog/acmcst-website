Call for Papers

ACMCST 2027 — International Conference | Béni Mellal, Morocco

Home › Call for Papers

The ACMCST 2027 invites researchers, academics, engineers, and industry professionals to submit original and unpublished contributions in the fields of advanced communications, modern circuits, and systems technologies. Papers should present significant, novel, and technically sound research.

All accepted papers will be published in Springer Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems and indexed in SCOPUS, INSPEC, WTI Frankfurt eG, zbMATH, and SCImago.

Instructions

Submission Guidelines

Paper Format

  • Papers must be written in English
  • Use Springer LNNS template (Word or LaTeX)
  • Length: 10–15 pages including references
  • All papers must be original and unpublished
  • Double-blind review process

Submission Process

  • Submit via the online submission system
  • PDF format required for submission
  • All papers will be peer-reviewed
  • At least one author must register
  • Accepted papers must be presented at the conference

Publication

  • Published by Springer LNNS
  • Indexed in SCOPUS & INSPEC
  • Also indexed in WTI Frankfurt eG
  • zbMATH and SCImago indexed
  • DOI assigned to each paper

Special Session

Special Session: Embedded Systems & Applications

This special session focuses on the latest advances in embedded systems design and their applications across various domains including automotive, industrial, medical, aerospace, and consumer electronics. It brings together researchers and industry experts to discuss innovative solutions for embedded systems.

Topics include:

›Real-time embedded systems (RTOS)
›Hardware/Software co-design
›FPGA and SoC design
›Low-power embedded systems
›Embedded AI and Machine Learning
›Embedded systems for IoT
›Embedded systems for automotive
›Embedded systems for medical devices
›Embedded systems for aerospace
›Cyber-physical systems
›Embedded security and safety
›Edge computing
